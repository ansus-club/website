Who We Are

Anandanagar Sanskriti Unnayan Samity is a community-based cultural organization. It focuses on preserving and promoting regional art, culture, and heritage through local festivals, educational programs, and community initiatives that strengthen neighborhood cultural identity.

Cultural Activities

Annual events including music, dance, and drama performances, collaborating with local artists and schools to highlight Bengal's folk traditions and contemporary creativity.

Community Engagement

Inclusive participation for youth and marginalized groups through workshops and educational outreach in art, literature, and performance.

Our Impact

Sustaining neighbourhood-level cultural life, supporting regional identity, civic engagement, and continuity of Bengal's diverse cultural practices in a rapidly urbanising environment.
